    Inkscape and Laser Cutter¶

    For this session notes, I refered to Sonam Pema Yangchen's Documentation 1 and Documentation 2

    Raster images are made of pixels. When you zoom in too much, they become blurry. Some examples include JPEG, PNG, and WEBP.

    Vector images are made using lines and shapes (math-based). You can zoom in as much as you want and they stay sharp. Some examples are SVG and EPS.

    Engraving and Vector¶

    Laser Cutter¶

    Engrave¶

    Engraving is when the laser only burns the top surface of the material to create a design (without cutting all the way through)

    Vector¶

    Vector cutting is when the laser follows a thin line to slice completely through the material.

    Kerf¶

    While vector cutting, one very important detail to consider is the kerf. Kerf is the amount of material that is removed when the laser cuts. To achieve accurate and precise cutting results, this must be taken into account.

    Making a Keychain¶

    To make a keychain, first thing we need to make is the shape of the keychain.

    Boundary¶

    to make boundary of the keychain we can use the polygon cursor to make different shapes. for this session we will make square.

    after selcting the square cursor, then we can click on any place in the workplace and drag to a different position.

    in the above image, on the panel above shows - Width (Horizontal Length), - Height (Vertical Length), - Rx and Ry will affect the corners.

    Fill and Stroke¶

    the above image shows the panel to manipulate fill and stock.

    1. Fill : This will add a color to the interior of the enclosed shape.
    2. Stroke : This will controll the visibility of the boundary.
      • inside each of this tab, if you click on the 'x' mark then that feature will be removed and the solid square will make that feature visible.
    3. Stroke-style : In this tab we can define thickness of the stroke.

    To make the boundary, we will turn off the fill and turn on the stroke.

    then make sure to turn on the stroke style

    then under the stroke style, click on the unit dropdown and select hairline.

    Dimensions¶

    Now to edit the dimension of the rectangle you can double click on the boundary line and then you can see the W, H, Rx, Ry

    • you can edit the dimension and change Rx or Ry to make the corners curve.

    keyhole¶

    Now to make a keyhole, in the same way as we made the rectangle we can select the circle and and make one on one of the edge.

    here on this image Rx and Ry is the Radius of the circle in x and y axis.

    Adding Text.¶

    On the left side of the interface, we can see the letter ' A ' which can enable us to write text.

    After selecting the text cursor then you can click and drag to write the text.

    Once after writing the text then you can click on the select cursor and adjust the text size

    After writing the text, you might have to adjust the fill and stroke of the text

    • make sure to turn off the stroke so that the text will not be cut through.

    Adding a image¶

    First import the image inside inkscape

    Then right click on the image and click bitmao trace

    Then on the right side panel the bitmap trace tab will appear

    1. Make sure it is selected to brightness cutoff
    2. Then click on apply

    Then once it is applied then we can see that in the image a trace is created and you can drag it out.

    now if you zoom in you will notice clear distintion between vector and raster image. And we will be using the vector one.

    And then you can drag the image to your keychain and delete the raster image
